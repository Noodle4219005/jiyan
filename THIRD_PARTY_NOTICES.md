## LXGW WenKai TC (heading font)

Jiyan bundles a subset of **LXGW WenKai TC** (Regular and Medium; about 6,700 common Traditional and Simplified
Chinese characters plus Latin and punctuation), converted to WOFF2 for use as the heading font.

Everything else Jiyan downloads at run time (speech and speaker models, Python packages) keeps its own licence.
